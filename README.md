supermarket-sales-dashboard

Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing 3 months of supermarket sales data across 3 branch cities in Myanmar. Built to answer: which product lines and cities drive revenue, and how is performance trending month over month?

Key Insights

- Food & Beverages generates the highest revenue of any product line, outperforming Health & Beauty by 14.13%
- February shows a -16.40% MoM revenue decline followed by strong March recovery, consistent with post-January seasonal patterns
- Naypyitaw leads all three branch cities in total revenue despite being a smaller city than Yangon
